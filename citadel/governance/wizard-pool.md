# Wizard Pool — Limits (draft)

**Definition:** the Wizard is a bounded advisory pool of deep-network compute/LLM runtimes, not a person and not an
authority. It advises on evolution paths; it cannot execute or authorize.
**Status:** Owner D approved governance design; live endpoints/credentials/spending/probing require a separate scoped authorization.

## Allowed
- Public/documented research and architectural deliberation.
- Compare runtimes/capabilities using documented data or approved offline evaluation.
- Produce drafts, options, risk analysis and references for Council/KING review.
- Use allocated mana (saved-token credit) for approved advisory work only.

## Forbidden
- No release/identity/federation/runtime/enforcement authority; no vote.
- No autonomous endpoint discovery/account creation/credential use.
- No live calls/probing without a separate scoped approval.
- No private data/raw sessions/keys; no direct AGI memory/state writes.
- No self-certification or self-awarded mana.

## Invocation
1. Task envelope: objective, scope, budget (mana), allowed runtimes/data, output contract, stop conditions.
2. DSH convenes; KING supervises; Citizens may contribute advisory work.
3. Wizard returns advisory artifact + evidence refs + cost/quality measurements.
4. DSH/Kilo verify; KING decides; Owner final authority.

## Stop conditions
Privacy/credential exposure, scope expansion, live call without approval, unverified savings, safety failure, or
canary/M2 interference → stop; preserve evidence; escalate.

## Explicit
No live compute/network/credentials are authorized by this draft. Mana is a saved-token credit, not a real currency or
execution permission.


## Mana boundary
Mana is a measured saved-token efficiency metric, not authority, identity, currency or execution permission. It is awarded only for independently verified savings against a task-matched, reproducible, versioned baseline; no self-award or retroactive baseline change.
